Tips for a clean recording
  • Quiet room, phone about 10–15 cm from the mouth, same distance every time.
  • Letters (l0…l27) are the letter with fatha, for example “ba”, “ta” — not the letter name. Say it clearly and alone.
  • Words: say the word calmly at teaching speed, without the -un ending unless it is shown.
  • Vowels (196 clips): short a / u / i, sukoon (a short stop with no vowel), long aa / uu / ee. You can skip these now and record them later: progress is saved on this device.
  • Silence is trimmed and the volume is levelled automatically. Re-record any clip to replace it.
  • Keys: Space = record/stop, ← → = previous/next, P = play.
